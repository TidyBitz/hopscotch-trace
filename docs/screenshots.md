## Probing and trends

Keep probing pings every hop once a second. The Ping view charts the hop you choose and logs every probe and every drop.

![The Ping view probing every hop of a route, with a chart of one hop's round trips](screenshots/ping-view.png)

Trends compares any two saved runs hop by hop and says where the time went.

![Trends for a target: round trip over time, two runs compared hop by hop, and a small chart per hop](screenshots/trends.png)
